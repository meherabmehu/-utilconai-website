AI and IoT for Modern Utility Infrastructure Construction<../index.htmlh2> <../index.htmldiv>

Utility construction presents unique operational challenges that differ significantly from conventional commercial construction. Construction activities frequently involve numerous subcontractors, specialized vehicles, heavy equipment, temporary storage yards, spool yards, staging areas, and continuously changing work fronts.<../index.htmlp>
